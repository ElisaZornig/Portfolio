The challenge

How do we make players want to keep exploring?

Together with my team, I developed a story-driven game set in Heemraadsplein in Rotterdam. You play as a bee collecting nectar to save the colony, while avoiding or defeating pigeons, spiders and a pest controller.

The game is designed for players who enjoy exploring, finding secrets and choosing their own route. Hidden objects and unlockable hats give them a reason to keep looking.

The outcome

An adventure you can play.

We presented Heemraadshive on an arcade cabinet at the final showcase. Visitors could play the game and explore its world for themselves. The game is also playable in a browser.

Inside the project

  • Collect nectar to save the bee colony.
  • Avoid or defeat enemies.
  • Find hidden objects and unlock hats.
