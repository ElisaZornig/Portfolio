The challenge

How can practising sign language become more interactive?

For the Gebarentaal bij Intake elective, we built a web app that allows students to practise independently. The existing workbook provided the basis for flashcards, interactive exercises and a sign language dictionary.

Working with the front-end and back-end teams, I helped create an environment that follows the visual identity of Rotterdam University of Applied Sciences. Students can track their progress, while teachers can add students and monitor their learning through an admin panel.

The outcome

From concept to learning experience.

We presented the app to the client during a live demo, which was positively received. The delivered platform provides a foundation for expanding the learning materials.

Inside the project

  • Flashcards, drag-and-drop tasks, fill-in exercises and multiple-choice questions.
  • A searchable sign language dictionary with filters and favourites.
  • Progress tracking and feedback during exercises.
  • HR Single Sign-On, with access managed by a teacher.
  • An admin panel for teachers.
