The foundation

Helping people take
their next step in education.

The Joke van Boxtel Fonds supports residents of Nieuwkoop who need help with the costs of studying. It offers interest-free study loans and donated laptops.

Established in memory of Joke van Boxtel, the fund continues her commitment to helping people develop their skills and independence.

My contribution

A real organisation.
A website built by me.

I built the website for the foundation independently. This was an opportunity to put my development skills to use outside a school assignment, on a public website with a practical purpose.

The focus

Make it clear what support is available, who it is for and how to take the next step.

The website

From understanding the fund
to starting an application.

01

Understand the support

The homepage introduces the fund and explains the two types of help: a study loan and a laptop.

02

Find the relevant information

Visitors can read the eligibility criteria before continuing to the application. The header also includes a language selector.

03

Take the next step

An online form brings together personal details, study information and the support requested. Contact and foundation documents are available on the site.

The outcome

A public website
with a practical purpose.

The finished website brings the fund's work, practical information and application form together. Visitors can move from the introduction to the information and form on the same page.
