The challenge

How can applying for work become simpler and more accessible?

For Open Hiring, we designed a platform that lets people apply without a CV or cover letter. The concept aims to reach people who face barriers in traditional recruitment, including people with low literacy skills or disabilities.

We designed the interface mobile-first, with straightforward navigation and recognisable icons. The design follows Open Hiring's visual identity and takes accessibility into account.

The outcome

A more approachable first step.

The result is a working prototype that explores a more accessible application process. The focus is on a clear user experience and a lower barrier to finding work.
